How it works

  1. Describe your organisation. The programme builder creates a profile in your browser: size, sector, Microsoft estate, current AI use, governance, adoption and constraints. Nothing is sent anywhere.
  2. Get a recommendation you can check. The profile is matched to an edition (Starter, Scale or Enterprise) and a maturity archetype, and each recommendation names the rule that produced it.
  3. Ask for the pack. In Microsoft Copilot Cowork, attach the profile and ask: "Design our Microsoft AI programme." The departments produce a coherent transformation pack: assessment, operating model, adoption and Champions programmes, CoE, opportunity process, agent-delivery route, governance, roadmap, ownership map and success measures.

Use it in Microsoft Copilot Cowork

  1. Download the departments you need from the downloads page: one plugin per department, each named copilotforge-<department>.
  2. Upload them in Cowork: Customize > Plugins > Upload plugin, and share with your team.
  3. Add the suggested custom instructions, then ask your questions. The Copilot Cowork guide has the details.

Proportionate at every scale

Starter

Small organisation or a department beginning adoption.

Simple roles, minimal governance overhead, safe use, high-value scenarios, practical templates and partner support where needed.

Scale

Mid-sized organisation, or adoption expanding beyond pilots.

Champions, repeatable intake, agent ownership, platform governance, portfolio visibility and formal measures.

Enterprise

Large or multi-business-unit organisation.

Federated governance, formal assurance, multiple platforms and environments, portfolio management, production operations and benefits realisation.

Governance rises with impact, reach, data sensitivity and autonomy, through five progressive risk tiers. A personal prompt pattern and a customer-facing production agent do not pass through the same gates.

Principles

  • Outcome before technology. The first decision is whether AI is appropriate at all.
  • Business-owned, AI-enabled. The business owns priorities, knowledge, outcomes, adoption and value; AI and IT teams enable.
  • Evidence over attendance. Champions are recognised for demonstrated capability and contribution.
  • Knowledge is infrastructure. Reliable AI depends on owned, current, classified knowledge.
  • Current facts come from sources. Availability, licensing and limits are cited from Microsoft sources, never asserted from memory.
  • Human accountability stays explicit. AI assists; people remain accountable for outcomes.
