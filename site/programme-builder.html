Programme builder

Describe your organisation and get a recommended edition, maturity archetype and programme, with the rule behind each recommendation. Then download the profile and give it to CopilotForge in Copilot Cowork. Everything runs in your browser: nothing you type is sent anywhere.

Keep it generic. Use an alias for the organisation and roles rather than names. Save the downloaded profile in your own OneDrive, attach it in Copilot Cowork and ask: "Design our Microsoft AI programme from this profile." You confirm the edition, archetype and ownership map before any document is written.
