Programmes

Programmes orchestrate departments into complete outcomes. They are the main way to use CopilotForge; departments and skills are the reusable parts beneath them. Each artefact names the skill responsible for it, the template it is written on, and how deep it goes in each edition.

AI Transformation

The superset programme. Orchestrates strategy, adoption, Champions, CoE, delivery, assurance, operations and value realisation into one coherent transformation pack (decision D10).

Lifecycle: Discover, Envision, Mobilise, Enable, Deliver, Assure, Launch, Measure, Scale, Improve

Includes: Microsoft AI Discovery, Copilot Adoption, Copilot Champions, AI Centre of Excellence, Agent Factory (when agents are emerging or the organisation is a Builder or beyond), AI Governance
