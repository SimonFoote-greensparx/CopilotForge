AI Strategy first version, draft

Transformation · Journey: Strategy · Lead role: AI Strategy Lead · Built in phase 1

Define the organisation's AI ambition, target outcomes, principles, operating model and roadmap, starting from business outcomes rather than products.

  • ai-strategy:ai-maturity-assessmentdraft

    Diagnoses an organisation's current AI maturity across strategy, people and adoption, knowledge, governance, platform and delivery capability, and value measurement, using observed evidence rather than self-scored opinion, and maps the result to a CopilotForge archetype. Use when a programme is starting, when leaders disagree about how ready they are, or when a strategy refresh needs an honest baseline.

    Lifecycle: Discover, Improve · Editions: starter, scale, enterprise

  • ai-strategy:ai-operating-modeldraft

    Designs the target operating model for AI - who decides, who builds, who supports and who owns value - choosing a central, hub-and-spoke or federated shape to suit the edition and archetype, and placing the Centre of Excellence within it. Use when AI work is spreading without clear ownership, when leaders ask how AI should be organised, or before a CoE is chartered.

    Lifecycle: Mobilise, Scale · Editions: starter, scale, enterprise

  • ai-strategy:ai-roadmapdraft

    Builds a prioritised, sequenced AI roadmap across the Adopt AI, Build with AI and Scale AI journeys and the lifecycle stages, showing dependencies, capacity, foundations and review points against the agreed outcomes. Use when leaders need a plan for the next twelve to twenty-four months, when initiatives compete for the same people, or when an existing roadmap has drifted from its outcomes.

    Lifecycle: Mobilise, Scale, Improve · Editions: starter, scale, enterprise

  • ai-strategy:ai-visiondraft

    Articulates the organisation's AI ambition - a deliberately chosen level of ambition, a short vision statement staff can repeat, a narrative of how work will change and a handful of guiding principles - agreed by the leadership team and written for the whole workforce. Use when leaders cannot say in a sentence why the organisation is adopting AI, when functions hold competing pictures of it, or before outcomes and a roadmap are set.

    Lifecycle: Discover, Envision · Editions: starter, scale, enterprise

  • ai-strategy:executive-sponsorshipdraft

    Secures and equips an active executive sponsor for the whole AI programme - choosing the right executive, agreeing time, decision rights and visible behaviours in a sponsor agreement, building a peer sponsor coalition and checking sponsor engagement each quarter. Use when an AI programme has no sponsor or only a nominal one, when the sponsor has gone quiet, or when the sponsor changes role.

    Lifecycle: Envision, Mobilise, Scale · Editions: starter, scale, enterprise

  • ai-strategy:investment-casedraft

    Builds the funding case for the AI programme as a whole across the Adopt, Build and Scale journeys - investment options, full cost of ownership by category, benefit ranges tied to strategic outcomes, staged funding tranches released at evidence gates and the funding model. Use when leaders must decide how much to invest in AI overall, when a budget round needs a programme request, or when funding was approved for licences alone.

    Lifecycle: Envision, Mobilise, Scale · Editions: starter, scale, enterprise

  • ai-strategy:platform-strategydraft

    Sets the organisation's strategic position on the Microsoft AI platform mix - Copilot Chat, Microsoft 365 Copilot, agents, Copilot Cowork, Copilot Code, Copilot Studio and Microsoft Foundry - giving each a posture, audience, owner, entry criteria and sequence. Use when leaders ask which Microsoft AI platforms the organisation should offer and to whom, when teams adopt platforms ad hoc, or when Microsoft change prompts a rethink.

    Lifecycle: Envision, Mobilise, Scale · Editions: starter, scale, enterprise

  • ai-strategy:strategic-outcomesdraft

    Turns an AI ambition into three to five measurable business outcomes, a short set of AI principles and an explicit statement of where AI will not be used, with clear expectations of the executive sponsor. Use when leaders say they want an AI strategy, when a programme has activity but no agreed outcomes, or when a sponsor needs to explain why the organisation is investing in AI.

    Lifecycle: Envision, Mobilise · Editions: starter, scale, enterprise

  • ai-strategy:strategy-refreshdraft

    Refreshes an existing AI strategy on a fixed cycle or a defined trigger, testing the vision, outcomes, platform position and roadmap against measured outcomes, a re-run maturity baseline, Microsoft product change and new regulation, and records what changed and why. Use when the annual strategy review is due, when outcomes are off track, or when a major Microsoft or regulatory change makes the current strategy doubtful.

    Lifecycle: Measure, Improve, Scale · Editions: starter, scale, enterprise

Opportunity Management first version, draft

Transformation · Journey: Strategy · Lead role: AI Portfolio Manager · Built in phase 1

Create a disciplined path from business problem to prioritised and governed initiative, including the decision about whether AI is the right answer at all.

  • opportunity-management:ai-suitabilitydraft

    Decides whether a framed business problem should be solved with AI at all, and if so which pattern fits - Copilot Chat, a Cowork task, an agent, Copilot Code, a Foundry solution or plain automation - with the reasoning recorded. Use when an idea arrives at intake, when a team has already picked a tool, or when a sponsor asks why AI is preferable to process change or training.

    Lifecycle: Envision, Deliver · Editions: starter, scale, enterprise

  • opportunity-management:backlog-managementdraft

    Sets up and operates the AI opportunity backlog as one visible board - choosing the tool, defining stages with entry and exit rules, mandatory fields per stage, work-in-progress limits, ageing rules, merges, archiving and a regular hygiene routine - so it stays the single route to resources. Use when there is no backlog or several competing lists, when the board is stale or cluttered, or when nobody trusts its status fields.

    Lifecycle: Mobilise, Deliver, Measure · Editions: starter, scale, enterprise

  • opportunity-management:business-casedraft

    Writes a proportionate business case for one significant AI initiative - choosing no case, a light case or a full case by cost, risk and reach, then setting out options including a non-AI option, build and run costs, benefits from the value hypothesis, risks, the ask and the decision. Use when a single agent, automation or engineered solution needs funding or approval beyond delegated limits, or when a sponsor asks for a case before committing.

    Lifecycle: Envision, Mobilise · Editions: starter, scale, enterprise

  • opportunity-management:effort-assessmentdraft

    Estimates what an AI opportunity will really take - build effort by pattern, the scarce skills and owners it needs, its dependencies on knowledge, data, platforms and reviewers, and the ongoing run cost after release - as ranges with stated confidence. Use when an opportunity needs a feasibility score, when a team says an agent will take a week, or when nobody has asked who will run and pay for a solution after launch.

    Lifecycle: Envision, Mobilise · Editions: starter, scale, enterprise

  • opportunity-management:idea-capturedraft

    Designs and runs the organisation's open route for staff to submit AI ideas - the submission channels, a short form, acknowledgement and triage timings, duplicate checks, idea campaigns and feedback to every submitter - so ideas reach the backlog instead of side requests. Use when there is no obvious place to send AI ideas, when ideas arrive through managers and chat threads and get lost, or when submissions have dried up.

    Lifecycle: Discover, Envision · Editions: starter, scale, enterprise

  • opportunity-management:opportunity-prioritisationdraft

    Scores and balances a backlog of framed AI opportunities on value, feasibility, risk, readiness of knowledge and ownership, and strategic fit, and shapes a portfolio of quick wins and strategic bets. Admits nothing without an owner, problem, outcome, risk tier and suitability decision. Use when ideas outnumber capacity, when a steering group must choose what goes next, or when the backlog has lost its link to outcomes.

    Lifecycle: Envision, Mobilise, Scale · Editions: starter, scale, enterprise

  • opportunity-management:pilot-selectiondraft

    Chooses which AI opportunities to pilot and designs each pilot so it can prove or disprove its value hypothesis - a representative cohort, a comparison, a fixed duration, success and stop thresholds set in advance, quality checks, risk containment and an exit plan. Use when an opportunity is ready to test with real users, when pilots keep running indefinitely, or when a pilot ended and nobody could tell whether it worked.

    Lifecycle: Mobilise, Deliver, Measure · Editions: starter, scale, enterprise

  • opportunity-management:portfolio-balancingdraft

    Reviews the whole AI opportunity portfolio, planned and in flight, and rebalances its mix across quick wins, strategic bets and foundation work, risk-tier exposure, scarce owner and reviewer capacity, business units, outcomes and platforms, then decides what to start, pause, accelerate or stop. Use when the portfolio feels lopsided, when reviewers or owners are overloaded, or at each quarterly portfolio review.

    Lifecycle: Mobilise, Scale, Improve · Editions: starter, scale, enterprise

  • opportunity-management:problem-framingdraft

    Turns a raw idea or tool request into a framed business problem with a named business owner, affected roles, current cost, expected outcome and baseline, and politely sends solution-first requests back to the problem. Use when an idea arrives at intake, when someone asks for an agent or Copilot feature before saying what is wrong, or when a pilot cannot say what it was meant to improve.

    Lifecycle: Envision · Editions: starter, scale, enterprise

  • opportunity-management:risk-classificationdraft

    Assigns a progressive risk tier from 0 to 4 to an AI opportunity or solution by rating impact, reach, data sensitivity and autonomy, then derives the minimum governance posture and the reviewer gates it must pass, and re-classifies when scope changes. Use when an idea has a chosen pattern, when a solution is about to be shared more widely, or when someone asks which reviews a build needs.

    Lifecycle: Envision, Assure, Scale · Editions: starter, scale, enterprise

  • opportunity-management:scale-or-stopdraft

    Makes the evidence-based decision after a pilot or first release to scale, iterate, pivot or stop, judged against the value hypothesis, adoption, quality and cost, and treats a well-evidenced stop as a success. Use when a pilot reaches its review date, when a sponsor wants to roll a solution out wider, or when a live initiative has been running for months without anyone deciding its future.

    Lifecycle: Measure, Scale, Improve · Editions: scale, enterprise

  • opportunity-management:value-hypothesisdraft

    States the expected value of a framed AI opportunity as a testable hypothesis before anything is built - the mechanism of change, the measure, its baseline, an expected range, the date it should show, leading indicators and the result that would disprove it. Use when an opportunity is about to be prioritised or piloted, when a team claims an agent will save time without saying how, or when nobody can say what would count as failure.

    Lifecycle: Envision, Mobilise · Editions: starter, scale, enterprise

Value Realisation first version, draft

Transformation · Journey: Measure · Lead role: Benefits Realisation Lead · Built in phase 3

Define baselines, measures and ownership so adoption and deployed solutions create demonstrable value.

  • value-realisation:adoption-to-value-metricsdraft

    Builds the measurement chain from adoption to benefit - usage, then scenario behaviour, then work outcome, then benefit - choosing leading indicators for their link to a named benefit and diagnosing where the chain breaks, so usage is never reported as value. Use when reports show active users but no outcomes, when a sponsor treats adoption as return, or when early warning is needed before benefits can be measured.

    Lifecycle: Mobilise, Launch, Measure, Improve · Editions: starter, scale, enterprise

  • value-realisation:baseline-designdraft

    Designs the baselines that must be captured before an AI change goes live - exact measure definitions, method, window, data source, sample and a comparison group where fair - so benefits can be proven later rather than asserted. Use when a launch or pilot date is set and nothing has been measured, when baselines rest on memory or vendor figures, or when seasonal or parallel change would make a simple before-and-after misleading.

    Lifecycle: Envision, Mobilise, Deliver, Measure · Editions: starter, scale, enterprise

  • value-realisation:benefit-hypothesisdraft

    Turns an approved value hypothesis into formal benefit statements, each with one change, one measure, a direction, a baseline, a target range, timing, an owner role, the enabling changes it depends on and any expected dis-benefits, ready for the benefits register. Use when an opportunity has been approved, when a case lists vague benefits such as efficiency or better insight, or when two solutions claim the same benefit.

    Lifecycle: Envision, Mobilise, Deliver · Editions: starter, scale, enterprise

  • value-realisation:benefit-ownerdraft

    Assigns an accountable business owner role to every benefit, agrees what ownership means - influencing the change, signing off baseline and target, deciding how released capacity is used, reporting and escalating - and sets up the support, hand-over and escalation owners need. Use when benefits sit with IT or the CoE, when an owner has moved role, or when owners have signed up to benefits they cannot influence.

    Lifecycle: Mobilise, Deliver, Measure, Improve · Editions: starter, scale, enterprise

  • value-realisation:benefit-realisation-plandraft

    Plans how AI benefits will be realised over time - the benefits register, each benefit's profile by period, the enabling changes and decisions as milestones, measurement points, owners, review cadence, tolerances and closure rules - so value is tracked to closure rather than declared at launch. Use when a case is approved, when benefits have no dates or tracking, or when a portfolio needs one register and review rhythm.

    Lifecycle: Mobilise, Deliver, Launch, Measure, Improve · Editions: starter, scale, enterprise

  • value-realisation:financial-benefitsdraft

    Quantifies the financial benefits of AI work with the finance function, classifying each as cashable, non-cashable, cost avoidance or revenue, agreeing the valuation method, evidence and budget line, and keeping released time apart from cash until a decision redeploys or removes cost. Use when a case or CFO needs money figures, when hours saved are being multiplied into savings, or when benefits must be banked in budgets.

    Lifecycle: Envision, Mobilise, Measure, Improve · Editions: starter, scale, enterprise

  • value-realisation:non-financial-benefitsdraft

    Measures AI benefits that should not be forced into money - customer and employee experience, risk and compliance reduction, decision quality, resilience and organisational capability - with defined indicators, baselines, owners and an evidence standard for each, and no spurious cash value. Use when a case rests on experience or risk reduction, when finance dismisses soft benefits, or when capability gains need evidence a sponsor will accept.

    Lifecycle: Envision, Mobilise, Measure, Improve · Editions: starter, scale, enterprise

  • value-realisation:post-implementation-reviewdraft

    Runs a post-implementation review an agreed period after an AI solution or programme goes live, comparing actual outcomes, costs, adoption, quality and risk with the approved case, explaining each variance, capturing lessons and agreeing actions for benefits still to come. Use when a live solution reaches its review date, when a funding body asks whether the case was delivered, or when lessons should improve how future cases are estimated.

    Lifecycle: Measure, Scale, Improve · Editions: starter, scale, enterprise

  • value-realisation:productivity-measurementdraft

    Measures the productivity effect of Copilot, agents, Cowork tasks and automation credibly - throughput, cycle time and capacity released from work data, sampled timing and comparison groups - and tracks where released capacity went, keeping self-reported hours saved as a perception measure only. Use when an hours-saved survey is challenged, when a sponsor asks what people did with saved time, or when a pilot must show a productivity effect.

    Lifecycle: Deliver, Launch, Measure, Improve · Editions: starter, scale, enterprise

  • value-realisation:quality-measurementdraft

    Measures how AI-assisted work changes business quality after launch - accuracy, rework, errors reaching customers, compliance exceptions and consistency - through sampled review against a rubric, operational defect data and comparison with unassisted work. Use when a sponsor asks whether AI-assisted work is better or only faster, when rework or complaints rise after launch, or when productivity gains need a quality guard.

    Lifecycle: Deliver, Launch, Measure, Improve · Editions: starter, scale, enterprise

  • value-realisation:roi-modeldraft

    Builds a transparent return-on-investment model for an AI initiative or portfolio - full costs across licences, consumption, build, run, review effort, support and change, benefits by class and timing, low, central and high ranges, sensitivity to key assumptions and an assumptions log. Use when a case or renewal needs a return figure, when an ROI number cannot be traced to its inputs, or when estimated and actual return must be compared.

    Lifecycle: Envision, Mobilise, Measure, Scale, Improve · Editions: starter, scale, enterprise

  • value-realisation:value-dashboarddraft

    Specifies a sponsor value dashboard for the AI portfolio across value, adoption, capability and governance, with each tile's measure, method, confidence, baseline, owner and source, plus the data flow from the benefits register, refresh, access, minimum group size and caveats, ready for a report builder. Use when sponsors need one view of realised value, when a dashboard shows usage alone, or when a chosen scorecard now needs building.

    Lifecycle: Measure, Scale, Improve · Editions: starter, scale, enterprise

    Lifecycle: Mobilise, Enable, Deliver, Assure, Launch, Measure · Editions: starter, scale, enterprise

  • programme-management:stage-gatesdraft

    Defines the stage gates of an AI programme along the CopilotForge lifecycle - from mobilisation through enablement, delivery, launch and scale to closure - with the evidence each gate needs, who signs it off, how reviewer findings carry into it, and the outcomes go, conditional go, hold or stop. Use when a programme needs control points, when gates have become ceremonies, or when a sponsor asks what must be true before the next stage.

    Lifecycle: Mobilise, Enable, Deliver, Assure, Launch, Measure, Scale · Editions: starter, scale, enterprise

  • programme-management:stakeholder-governancedraft

    Governs how stakeholders take part in an AI programme's decisions - decision rights by decision type, who must be consulted or must approve (security, data protection, legal, HR, employee representatives, business units), escalation routes and time limits, and an engagement plan for governance stakeholders. Use when nobody knows who can approve a change, when decisions stall between functions, or when a required voice was left out.

    Lifecycle: Mobilise, Deliver, Assure, Launch · Editions: starter, scale, enterprise

  • programme-management:supplier-managementdraft

    Selects and manages the partners and suppliers an AI programme relies on - deciding what to source, setting outcome-based requirements and criteria, testing Microsoft AI capability claims on evidence, structuring work packages, knowledge transfer and exit, and reviewing delivery at service level. Use when a programme needs a Microsoft partner or ISV, when a supplier underdelivers, or when the organisation risks depending on a partner for good.
