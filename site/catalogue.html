AI Strategy active

Transformation · Journey: Strategy · Lead role: AI Strategy Lead · Built in phase 1

Define the organisation's AI ambition, target outcomes, principles, operating model and roadmap, starting from business outcomes rather than products.

  • ai-strategy:ai-maturity-assessmentdraft

    Diagnoses an organisation's current AI maturity across strategy, people and adoption, knowledge, governance, platform and delivery capability, and value measurement, using observed evidence rather than self-scored opinion, and maps the result to a CopilotForge archetype. Use when a programme is starting, when leaders disagree about how ready they are, or when a strategy refresh needs an honest baseline.

    Lifecycle: Discover, Improve · Editions: starter, scale, enterprise

  • ai-strategy:ai-operating-modeldraft

    Designs the target operating model for AI - who decides, who builds, who supports and who owns value - choosing a central, hub-and-spoke or federated shape to suit the edition and archetype, and placing the Centre of Excellence within it. Use when AI work is spreading without clear ownership, when leaders ask how AI should be organised, or before a CoE is chartered.

    Lifecycle: Mobilise, Scale · Editions: starter, scale, enterprise

  • ai-strategy:ai-roadmapdraft

    Builds a prioritised, sequenced AI roadmap across the Adopt AI, Build with AI and Scale AI journeys and the lifecycle stages, showing dependencies, capacity, foundations and review points against the agreed outcomes. Use when leaders need a plan for the next twelve to twenty-four months, when initiatives compete for the same people, or when an existing roadmap has drifted from its outcomes.

    Lifecycle: Mobilise, Scale, Improve · Editions: starter, scale, enterprise

  • ai-strategy:strategic-outcomesdraft

    Turns an AI ambition into three to five measurable business outcomes, a short set of AI principles and an explicit statement of where AI will not be used, with clear expectations of the executive sponsor. Use when leaders say they want an AI strategy, when a programme has activity but no agreed outcomes, or when a sponsor needs to explain why the organisation is investing in AI.

    Lifecycle: Envision, Mobilise · Editions: starter, scale, enterprise

Opportunity Management active

Transformation · Journey: Strategy · Lead role: AI Portfolio Manager · Built in phase 1

Create a disciplined path from business problem to prioritised and governed initiative, including the decision about whether AI is the right answer at all.

  • opportunity-management:ai-suitabilitydraft

    Decides whether a framed business problem should be solved with AI at all, and if so which pattern fits - Copilot Chat, a Cowork task, an agent, Copilot Code, a Foundry solution or plain automation - with the reasoning recorded. Use when an idea arrives at intake, when a team has already picked a tool, or when a sponsor asks why AI is preferable to process change or training.

    Lifecycle: Envision, Deliver · Editions: starter, scale, enterprise

  • opportunity-management:opportunity-prioritisationdraft

    Scores and balances a backlog of framed AI opportunities on value, feasibility, risk, readiness of knowledge and ownership, and strategic fit, and shapes a portfolio of quick wins and strategic bets. Admits nothing without an owner, problem, outcome, risk tier and suitability decision. Use when ideas outnumber capacity, when a steering group must choose what goes next, or when the backlog has lost its link to outcomes.

    Lifecycle: Envision, Mobilise, Scale · Editions: starter, scale, enterprise

  • opportunity-management:problem-framingdraft

    Turns a raw idea or tool request into a framed business problem with a named business owner, affected roles, current cost, expected outcome and baseline, and politely sends solution-first requests back to the problem. Use when an idea arrives at intake, when someone asks for an agent or Copilot feature before saying what is wrong, or when a pilot cannot say what it was meant to improve.

    Lifecycle: Envision · Editions: starter, scale, enterprise

  • opportunity-management:risk-classificationdraft

    Assigns a progressive risk tier from 0 to 4 to an AI opportunity or solution by rating impact, reach, data sensitivity and autonomy, then derives the minimum governance posture and the reviewer gates it must pass, and re-classifies when scope changes. Use when an idea has a chosen pattern, when a solution is about to be shared more widely, or when someone asks which reviews a build needs.

    Lifecycle: Envision, Assure, Scale · Editions: starter, scale, enterprise

  • opportunity-management:scale-or-stopdraft

    Makes the evidence-based decision after a pilot or first release to scale, iterate, pivot or stop, judged against the value hypothesis, adoption, quality and cost, and treats a well-evidenced stop as a success. Use when a pilot reaches its review date, when a sponsor wants to roll a solution out wider, or when a live initiative has been running for months without anyone deciding its future.

    Lifecycle: Measure, Scale, Improve · Editions: scale, enterprise

Copilot Adoption active

Adoption · Journey: Adopt · Lead role: Adoption Lead · Built in phase 1

Design and run organisation-wide or business-unit Microsoft AI adoption programmes, from readiness and scenarios through launch, reinforcement and measurement.

  • copilot-adoption:adoption-measurementdraft

    Defines how Copilot adoption is measured across usage, capability, behaviour change and business outcomes, with baselines set before launch, team or role-level reporting above a minimum group size, and feedback loops that change the programme. Use when setting adoption measures, when a sponsor asks whether Copilot is working, or when someone proposes tracking individual usage.

    Lifecycle: Discover, Measure, Improve · Editions: starter, scale, enterprise

  • copilot-adoption:adoption-readinessdraft

    Assesses whether an organisation or business unit is ready to adopt Microsoft Copilot across sponsorship, licences assigned versus used, IT-led technical enablement, data and permissions hygiene, approved-use guidance, support and communications, and records the gaps to close before launch. Use when licences have been bought or are about to be, before a launch date is fixed, or when early use has stalled.

    Lifecycle: Discover, Mobilise · Editions: starter, scale, enterprise

  • copilot-adoption:launch-plandraft

    Plans a phased Copilot launch with onboarding by persona, executive and manager enablement, reinforcement through office hours and Champions, a support model and a feedback loop that keeps improving the programme. Use when readiness gaps are known and a rollout needs sequencing, when a big-bang launch is being proposed, or when a launch happened but nothing followed it.

    Lifecycle: Mobilise, Enable, Launch · Editions: starter, scale, enterprise

  • copilot-adoption:scenario-discoverydraft

    Segments the workforce into personas such as desk, hybrid and frontline workers, discovers role-based Copilot scenarios with business owners, prioritises them by value and frequency, and builds a scenario catalogue with prompt patterns that users can verify. Use when planning what people will actually do with Copilot, when usage is generic and shallow, or when a new team or role joins the rollout.

    Lifecycle: Discover, Envision, Enable · Editions: starter, scale, enterprise

Champions active

Adoption · Journey: Adopt · Lead role: Champions Programme Owner · Built in phase 1

Create evidence-led Champion programmes that turn curiosity into capability and capability into contribution, with clear boundaries on what Champions do and do not do.

  • champions:capability-frameworkdraft

    Defines the three Champion capabilities - Chat, productivity and agents - each with an objective, a training option, an evidence option, verification criteria and an outcome, so training is one route rather than the gate, and maps learning sessions open to the wider business onto the journey. Use when setting what a Champion must show, when status needs to mean something, or when experienced colleagues want to skip training.

    Lifecycle: Enable, Measure · Editions: starter, scale, enterprise

  • champions:champion-challengesdraft

    Designs the Champion Challenge - one practical contribution after the three capabilities are verified, such as an envisioning workshop, team training, a governed business agent, a prompt library, a departmental playbook or support for the Centre of Excellence - with a safety and scope check and assessment on impact, reusability, quality and strategic alignment. Use when Champions are ready to contribute or a challenge idea needs checking.

    Lifecycle: Enable, Deliver, Scale · Editions: scale, enterprise

  • champions:champion-programme-designdraft

    Designs an evidence-led Copilot Champions programme as a progression system - purpose, the six-stage journey, operating boundaries, its place between the adoption programme and the Centre of Excellence, sponsorship, agreed time, community, tracker and self-service guidance. Use when starting or replicating a Champion network, when Champions have become an unpaid help desk, or when status means attendance.

    Lifecycle: Mobilise, Enable · Editions: starter, scale, enterprise

  • champions:evidence-assessmentdraft

    Sets up fair, consistent assessment of Champion evidence against each capability's verification criteria and of Champion Challenges, by trained assessors who assess the evidence rather than the person, keep a restricted progress tracker, give useful feedback, handle appeals and never rank anyone. Use when submissions start arriving, when assessors disagree, when progress status is unclear or when a decision is challenged.

    Lifecycle: Enable, Assure · Editions: starter, scale, enterprise

  • champions:recognition-and-progressiondraft

    Designs how Champions progress through the six-stage journey to recognised Champion, with recognition linked to demonstrated contribution, access to advanced materials, pilots and specialist pathways, renewal, a regular programme health check and programme-level measures such as milestone conversion. Use when designing Champion recognition, when status has gone stale, or when a sponsor asks whether the programme is healthy.

    Lifecycle: Measure, Scale, Improve · Editions: starter, scale, enterprise

Microsoft 365 Agents active

Microsoft AI delivery · Journey: Build · Lead role: Agent Product Owner · Built in phase 1

Turn organisational knowledge and repeatable information needs into appropriately scoped, owned and tested agents with a managed lifecycle.

  • m365-agents:agent-release-readinessdraft

    Runs the release gate for an agent, confirming owner, support route, sharing scope matched to risk tier, reviewer evidence, communications, monitoring, review date and retirement criteria, and registers the agent in the solution register. Use when an agent has passed testing and someone wants to share it, when its audience is about to widen, or when a live agent has no clear owner.

    Lifecycle: Assure, Launch, Improve · Editions: starter, scale, enterprise

  • m365-agents:agent-testingdraft

    Builds a proportionate test plan and a small evaluation set of real user questions with expected answers, covering groundedness, refusal and out-of-scope behaviour, permission trimming across different users and regression after change, against a threshold the business owner agrees. Use when an agent is ready for testing, before widening its audience, or after its instructions or knowledge change.

    Lifecycle: Deliver, Assure, Improve · Editions: starter, scale, enterprise

  • m365-agents:agent-use-case-designdraft

    Turns an approved agent opportunity into an agent brief covering audience, purpose, scope and exclusions, instructions approach, knowledge sources, actions, the build-or-reuse decision, owner, support and success measures. Use when an idea has passed suitability as an agent, when a maker wants to start building, or when an existing agent needs its scope rewritten.

    Lifecycle: Envision, Deliver · Editions: starter, scale, enterprise

  • m365-agents:knowledge-source-readinessdraft

    Assesses each knowledge source an agent will ground on for owner, currency, accuracy, permissions and oversharing, classification and review cycle, and records a ready, ready-with-conditions or not-ready verdict. Use when an agent brief names its sources, when a maker wants to add a site or library as knowledge, or when an agent starts giving outdated or surprising answers.

    Lifecycle: Deliver, Assure, Improve · Editions: starter, scale, enterprise

Security and Privacy Reviewer-class active

Assurance and operations · Journey: Govern · Lead role: Security and Privacy Reviewer · Built in phase 1

Reviewer-class department that assesses identity, access, data exposure, connectors, privacy and retention for AI solutions and blocks release while material risks are unresolved.

  • security-privacy:connector-reviewdraft

    Reviews the connectors, actions, tools, MCP servers and APIs an agent or automation uses, covering user versus maker authentication, least privilege, data policies, data leaving the tenant and confirmation for write actions, and classifies each finding as blocking or advisory with a fix. Use when an agent gains an action or tool, when a flow uses a new connector, or when a maker shares a connection.

    Lifecycle: Deliver, Assure, Launch, Improve · Editions: starter, scale, enterprise

  • security-privacy:data-exposure-reviewdraft

    Reviews what content an AI solution can reach and reveal - oversharing, permissions, sensitivity labels, the agent's sharing scope, web and public knowledge, and where outputs travel - and classifies each finding as blocking or advisory with a fix. Use when an agent or Copilot rollout reaches its security gate, when a maker adds a large knowledge source, or when sensitive content surfaces in answers.

    Lifecycle: Assure, Launch, Improve · Editions: starter, scale, enterprise

  • security-privacy:privacy-assessmentdraft

    Assesses personal data in an AI solution for lawful basis, purpose limitation, transparency, data minimisation, retention of prompts, responses and transcripts, DPIA screening under UK GDPR and ICO guidance, and people-related uses, classifying findings as blocking or advisory. Use when an agent or AI solution processes personal data, when transcripts are kept, or when AI outputs inform decisions about people.

    Lifecycle: Envision, Assure, Launch, Improve · Editions: starter, scale, enterprise

AI Centre of Excellence active

Spanning · Journey: Scale · Lead role: AI CoE Lead · Built in phase 1

Orchestrate a business-owned AI Centre of Excellence covering charter, operating model, team, governance, opportunity flow, knowledge, cadence, RACI and value measurement.

  • ai-centre-of-excellence:coe-cadencedraft

    Sets the meeting cadence and terms of reference for an AI CoE - a weekly working group, monthly steering where the sponsor decides, a quarterly strategy review and a community forum - with agendas, a decision log and sizing by edition. Use when a new CoE needs its forums set up, when meetings have become status updates that decide nothing, or when decisions are being taken in corridors and nobody can find them later.

    Lifecycle: Mobilise, Scale, Measure, Improve · Editions: starter, scale, enterprise

  • ai-centre-of-excellence:coe-charterdraft

    Drafts the charter for an AI Centre of Excellence - purpose, who owns what between the business, AI enablement and the sponsor, scope across the six pillars, decision rights, launch commitments and what the CoE is not - or the lighter terms of an AI working group. Use when leadership asks for an AI CoE, when a group has drifted into a reporting forum or request queue, or when a sponsor needs one page to agree before people are appointed.

    Lifecycle: Mobilise, Scale · Editions: starter, scale, enterprise

  • ai-centre-of-excellence:coe-governance-modeldraft

    Writes a proportionate AI governance framework and acceptable-use starter - five governance areas, progressive risk tiers 0 to 4 with minimum controls, an agent portfolio register with business, knowledge and technical owners, and reviewer gates - so low-risk experimentation is not blocked. Use when there is no approved AI policy, when departments build agents with no register or owner, or when governance is so heavy people work around it.

    Lifecycle: Mobilise, Assure, Launch, Improve · Editions: starter, scale, enterprise

  • ai-centre-of-excellence:coe-kpi-frameworkdraft

    Builds a KPI framework and scorecard for an AI CoE across adoption, capability, portfolio flow, quality, governance and value, each measure with a baseline, target, source and owner, rolled up into a four-quadrant sponsor dashboard at team level. Use when a sponsor asks how they will know AI is working, when reporting relies on licence counts or hours saved, or when steering needs a scorecard before its first value review.

    Lifecycle: Measure, Scale, Improve · Editions: starter, scale, enterprise

  • ai-centre-of-excellence:coe-operating-modeldraft

    Designs how an AI Centre of Excellence runs - a business-owned structure with sponsor, lead, core team, four Champion roles and central AI enablement support, its shape by edition, the collaboration space, the intake-to-delivery flow and a phased launch. Use when a charter is agreed and the CoE now needs people, routes and a workable flow, or when an existing CoE has become a bottleneck.

    Lifecycle: Mobilise, Scale, Improve · Editions: starter, scale, enterprise

  • ai-centre-of-excellence:coe-racidraft

    Builds a role-based RACI for AI work across the opportunity lifecycle, governance decisions, knowledge ownership, release and value, with exactly one Accountable per activity and the business accountable for outcomes. Use when nobody can say who approves an agent, when IT is being held to account for business benefits, or when a new CoE needs clear responsibilities before its first steering meeting.

    Lifecycle: Mobilise, Scale, Improve · Editions: starter, scale, enterprise
