# Code of conduct

CopilotForge welcomes anyone who wants to help organisations use AI well.

- Be respectful and constructive. Critique ideas, not people.
- Assume good intent, and ask before you assume.
- Do not share anyone's confidential, client or employer information.
- Harassment, discrimination and personal attacks are not tolerated.

The maintainer may remove content or contributors that break these rules. Raise concerns privately through the
repository's Security tab reporting route or by contacting the maintainer through GitHub.
