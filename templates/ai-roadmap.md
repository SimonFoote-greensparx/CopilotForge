# AI roadmap

Template id: `ai-roadmap`
Responsible skills: `ai-strategy:strategic-outcomes` (sections 1 to 4) and `ai-strategy:ai-roadmap` (sections 5 to 9)

Use this template to record the organisation's strategic AI outcomes, principles and exclusions, and the sequenced
roadmap that delivers them. Outcomes describe business results, never products. Any figure the organisation has not
measured stays marked `[To confirm: ...]`.

## 1. Strategic outcomes

current maturity baseline.
