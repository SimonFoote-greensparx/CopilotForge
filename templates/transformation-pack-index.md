# AI transformation pack index and success measures

Template id: `transformation-pack-index`

Responsible skill: `organisation-tailoring:transformation-pack`. Edition and archetype come from
`organisation-tailoring:edition-and-archetype`; accountable roles from `organisation-tailoring:ownership-map`.

How to use this template: this is the front page of your organisation's pack. List every artefact the tailoring plan
selects, at the depth your edition sets, with the skill and template responsible. Mark every fact your profile did not
supply as `[To confirm: ...]`. On a refresh, complete section 7 and agree it with the sponsor before replacing any
document.
