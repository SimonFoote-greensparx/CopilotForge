# AI programme ownership map

Template id: `ownership-map`

Responsible skill: `organisation-tailoring:ownership-map`.

How to use this template: list every department the programme engages, with its accountable role. Write a named owner
only where your organisation has declared one; otherwise write "to confirm". Describe gaps as role gaps, never as
judgements about people. Store the completed map in your organisation's own storage with access limited to the
programme team.
