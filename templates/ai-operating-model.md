## 3. Ownership rules

These hold whatever the shape. Record any local refinement.

- The business owns outcomes, priorities, knowledge, adoption and benefits; IT and AI teams enable.
- Reviewer gates are run once and reused across units.
- Every live solution has a named owner and a support route.
- A maker never approves their own release at tier 2 or above.
