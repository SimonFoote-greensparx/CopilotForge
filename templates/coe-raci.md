# AI CoE RACI

Template id: `coe-raci`

Responsible skill: `ai-centre-of-excellence:coe-raci`.

How to use this template: rename the role columns to your organisation's role titles (never personal names), add or
remove rows, then fill each cell with R (responsible), A (accountable), C (consulted) or I (informed). Every row has
exactly one A. Reviewers are C or hold a gate, never A for what they review. Champions are never A for approval or
release. Mark roles that do not yet exist `[To confirm: role to be appointed]`.
