# Persona and scenario catalogue

Template id: `scenario-catalogue`

Responsible skill: `copilot-adoption:scenario-discovery`.

Use this catalogue to record the personas in scope and the role-based scenarios each will use. Every fact the
organisation must supply is marked `[To confirm: ...]`; replace the marker when the fact is confirmed, and never fill
it with a guess. Example rows are fictional and are there to be replaced or deleted. Every published scenario has a
verification step and an owner.

## 3. Scenario scoring

Score each criterion 1 to 3. Prioritise scenarios scoring 10 or more with Verifiability and Data safety both at least 2.
Data safety of 1 means a security and privacy review before publication.
